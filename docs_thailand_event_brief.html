Thailand Wine & Liquor Event Market Entry Brief

Focus areas: Bangkok + Phuket

Purpose: Build a themed tasting-event portfolio where users interact based on persona and tier, then standardize event data for management decisions.

1) Market Snapshot (Desk Research)

  • Bangkok has stronger year-round B2B + premium F&B event density (trade shows, hotel festivals, roadshows, retail hubs).
  • Phuket has higher tourism-driven demand for experiential, sunset, beach-club, and resort wine/spirits tastings.
  • Near-term opportunity: hybrid model = anchor flagship + micro themed sessions + brand-led pop-ups.

4) Theme Library (Initial)

  • Old World vs New World: comparative stations and scorecards.
  • Tropical Pairing Lab: seafood, spice, and acidity matching (great for Phuket).
  • Asia Spirits Craft: rum, gin, whisky, and local craft stories.
  • Women in Wine & Spirits: founder-led panels + curated flights.
  • Zero/Low + Full Spectrum: inclusive beverage experience for mixed groups.

5) Suggested Pilot Rollout (90 Days)

  1. Month 1: partner mapping, legal/advertising compliance checklist, venue shortlist.
  2. Month 2: 2 micro-events in Bangkok + 1 resort-collab test in Phuket.
  3. Month 3: 1 signature themed event in each city, with CRM and conversion tracking.
